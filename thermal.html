Thermal Properties of Materials

Experimental characterization of materials through thermal measurements and physical analysis.

Objective

The aim of this laboratory work was to study the thermal behavior of different materials and determine their physical properties through experimental measurements.

Experimental Approach

Measurements were performed following a defined protocol in order to evaluate thermal expansion and heat transfer properties.

Physical Parameters Studied

  • Thermal expansion coefficient
  • Heat transfer properties
  • Material response to temperature variation
  • Measurement uncertainty

Data Analysis

Experimental data were processed and compared with theoretical models in order to evaluate the agreement between measurements and expected physical behavior.

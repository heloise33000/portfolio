Greenhouse Effect Experiment

Experimental study of thermal radiation and energy transfer through measurements of temperature variations and efficiency.

Objective

The objective of this experiment was to study the influence of greenhouse conditions on thermal energy transfer and analyze the efficiency of the system through experimental measurements.

Experimental Setup

The experiment involved temperature measurements, radiative exchanges analysis and comparison between different experimental configurations.

Measurements and Results

Temperature data were collected and analyzed to determine the impact of the experimental parameters on thermal behavior.

  • Temperature measurements
  • Energy transfer analysis
  • Experimental uncertainty evaluation
  • Scientific interpretation of results
